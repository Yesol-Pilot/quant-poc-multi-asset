# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

---

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

### Behavior we encourage

- 🤝 Respectful, constructive feedback (even when disagreeing)
- 🌍 Empathy and patience for non-native English/Korean speakers
- 📚 Citing sources, acknowledging others' work
- 🔬 Honest reporting of failures (this is a "Honest Failure" repo)
- 🧪 Asking for help, admitting "I don't know"
- 🇰🇷🌍 Bilingual contributions (Korean + English both welcome)

### Behavior that is unacceptable

- 🚫 Personal attacks, insulting comments, trolling
- 🚫 Sexual harassment, sexualized comments or imagery
- 🚫 Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- 🚫 Spam, off-topic promotion, MLM/get-rich-quick scams
- 🚫 Spreading FUD, market manipulation discussions
- 🚫 Investment advice / solicitation in any form
- 🚫 Promoting closed-source paid signal services

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Reporting

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent to:
- 📧 **dpthf1537@gmail.com** (private)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

### Consequences

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines:

1. **Correction** — private warning, request for public apology
2. **Warning** — no interaction with persons involved for a specified period
3. **Temporary Ban** — temporary ban from any interaction or contribution (1 week ~ 3 months)
4. **Permanent Ban** — permanent ban from any community interaction

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, code reviews, the project's Discord (if/when created), and in public spaces when representing the project (e.g., conference talks, Twitter/LinkedIn posts referencing the project).
