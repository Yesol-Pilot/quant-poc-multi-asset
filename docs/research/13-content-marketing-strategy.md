## Executive Summary (5 Core Findings)

1. **Content marketing has 3–10x the long-term ROI of viral launches.** A Substack at 500 subscribers + Twitter at 2000 followers + LinkedIn at 800 connections produces sustained visibility that ANY single HN front-page hit cannot match. The 12-week build should weight content marketing at **70% of effort**, viral launches at 30%.

2. **LinkedIn 2025–2026 algorithm change favors quant/finance content.** Carousels and PDFs generate 2–3x more dwell time than text/image (LinkedIn's new "Depth Score"). Comments are weighted 15x more than likes. **Quant methodology carousels (5-7 slides) are the highest-leverage format** — explain DSR, sensitivity sweeps, Avellaneda-Stoikov in visual deep dives. Owner can produce 1–2 per week with AI assistance, requires 15–30 min review per piece.

3. **Twitter quant influencer top tier is reachable via "reply guy" strategy.** Marcos López de Prado (@lopezdeprado), Ernest Chan (@echanQT / @chanep, 3,226 followers as of May 2024), Paul Wilmott (@wilmott) — all active and responding to thoughtful technical replies. The path: 100+ thoughtful replies on their tweets over 4 weeks → 20–50 follower-back rate → eventual quote-tweet by Tier 1 = 1000+ follower gain in 1 day.

4. **Substack finance category has 4–10% paid conversion (vs 3% Substack average).** Finance is one of the top 5 highest-earning Substack categories. Realistic 12-week target: 200–500 free subscribers + 5–20 paid ($5/month). The owner's "honest failure" + "AI-native PM in Korean retail" angle is a distinct enough niche to defend.

5. **Strategy Lead can autonomously draft 95% of content. Owner action = 15–30 min/week review + approval.** All weekly draft generation, scheduling, engagement reply, growth metric tracking can run autonomously. Owner's only mandatory action is final approval before publish (G2 gate per Neo Genesis Master Rules).

## Section 2 — LinkedIn Strategy (Korean + English Dual Track)

### 2.1 LinkedIn 2025–2026 Algorithm Reality

Major changes from research:
- **Post lifecycle:** Posts now stay in feed for **2–3 weeks** (vs 24h max in 2024) if conversation sustains.
- **Depth Score replaces vanity metrics:** Time spent on post (dwell time) is primary distribution signal.
- **Carousels / PDFs win:** 2–3x more dwell time than text/image. Video reach **plummeted** in 2025.
- **Comments weighted 15x more than likes.** Meaningful (15+ word) comments carry the most weight.
- **AI-generated content detection:** Low-value AI posts are deprioritized. Genuine expertise + unique perspective required.
- **Posting frequency:** 11+ posts/week gets massive algorithmic reward. Realistic for 1-person + AI assistance = 2–5 quality posts/week.
- **Best time:** 8–9 AM Tuesday/Wednesday (Korea: 4–5 PM KST same day, which works well for Korean recruiters checking after work).

## Section 6 — Cold Honest Risk Matrix

### 6.1 Burnout Risk

12 weeks × 3 hours/week × 3 channels = 100+ hours of content management. Even with Strategy Lead automation, owner review fatigue is real.

**Mitigation:**
- 2 weeks of buffer content drafted upfront (Week 0)
- Skip days allowed: 1 LinkedIn post/week is fine if Tue OR Thu only
- Substack: 1 post/week non-negotiable; if owner is on PTO, pre-draft 4 weeks ahead

### 6.2 Voice Inconsistency Risk

Risk: AI-generated content sounds AI-generated → LinkedIn algorithm deprioritizes (deprioritization explicit in 2025–2026 update).

**Mitigation:**
- Owner must read every post out loud before publishing for first 4 weeks (calibrates Strategy Lead to voice)
- Strategy Lead maintains "owner voice samples" document — every approved post gets added
- After 4 weeks, Strategy Lead drafts are 90%+ in owner voice already

### 6.3 Blind Review Risk (NeurIPS + TMLR)

Per active-tasks.md: **blind review HOLD on author-attributed promotion.**

**Mitigation:**
- All Substack/LinkedIn/Twitter posts about quant tech are FREE TO POST (no overlap with NeurIPS/TMLR identity).
- BUT: no public mention of NeurIPS or TMLR submission topics in content marketing until review ends.
- Strategy Lead must screen every post for review-hold violations before publish.

### 6.4 Pivot Risk

If after 4 weeks LinkedIn produces 0 traction → don't pivot, keep going. Algorithm rewards consistency.
If after 8 weeks Substack subscribers < 50 → re-examine niche framing.
If after 12 weeks Twitter followers < +100 → reply guy approach may need replacement (reduce reply count, increase original posting).

**Mitigation:** Owner G2 decision gate at Week 4, 8, 12. Strategy Lead pre-surfaces pivot options.
