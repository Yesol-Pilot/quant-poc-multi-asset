## Section 7 — Cold Honest Risks

### 7.1 Korean Community Self-Promotion Allergy

Cold reality: Korean retail quant community is MORE allergic to self-promotion than English-speaking equivalents. Reasons:
- Korean dev culture values 겸손 (humility) above self-promotion
- 카톡 / 디스코드 quant rooms have strict 1-strike rules
- Once banned from a 카톡방, banned permanently (no appeal)
- Reputation damage is permanent and visible (small community)

**Mitigation:**
- 4-week lurk minimum before posting
- Value-first (technical methodology) every post
- Zero links to own content in first 8 weeks
- Korean community = LONG-TERM relationship building, not short-term reach

### 7.2 Honest Failure Narrative Cultural Risk

Cold reality: Korean culture in finance is more loss-averse than English-speaking cultures. "I failed and learned" can be:
- Received well in dev community (Korean dev culture is more failure-tolerant)
- Received POORLY in traditional finance community (Korean finance culture treats failure as career-ending)

**Mitigation:**
- LinkedIn KR posts: emphasize "방법론적 학습" (methodological learning), NOT "I lost money"
- Korean Substack: deep technical content, framework-as-method-not-personal
- 카톡/디스코드: avoid personal failure stories; focus on data + methodology

### 7.3 38-Day PoC External-Change Pattern

Owner's prior PoC was killed by external infra changes (Binance API policy etc.). Same risk for Korean-track strategy:

- **카톡방 unilateral admin decisions** (one mod ban = community access lost)
- **네이버 카페 ownership changes** (long-term content lock-in risk)
- **Korean conference cancellations** (PyCon Korea 2020/2021 canceled due to COVID — could happen again)
- **Korean firm hiring freeze** (2025–2026 macro conditions)

**Mitigation:** Diversification — don't anchor Korean track on a single channel. Spread across LinkedIn KR + Substack KR + 1 카톡 + 1 디스코드 + KAIST connection + 2+ corporate targets.
